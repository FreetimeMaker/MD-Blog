## ****Luma Store****

### ****What's Luma Store?****

- **Luma Store is another App Store (Yes, I know there are many App Stores)**

### ****What does Luma Store offer?****

- **Luma Store offers 4 Integrated App Sources. For Example my own F-Droid Repo, F-Droid Official Repo, IzzyOnDroid F-Droid Repo and of course Luma Store's own Source.**

- **You can add Custom App Sources.**

- **You can install Open-Source Apps and proprietary Apps via Luma Store's own Source.**

- **As a developer you can easily add your own App via the Developer Dashboard that will be reviewed by myself at the moment. It doesn't matter if you got Open-Source or Closed-Source Apps.**

### ****Where can I add my own Apps to Luma Store?****

**Go to [https://luma.free-time.me](https://luma.free-time.me) or click [here](https://luma.free-time.me) to get to the Developer Dashboard.**

### ****Does Luma Store offer Analytics or any other things that Google Play or Apple App Store have?****

**No, Luma Store does not offer Analytics nor any other things that Google Play or Apple App Store have. Luma Store respects the User and maybe in the Future Luma Store will offer that, but not at the moment.**

### ****Will Luma Store get more Helpers or an Automation?****

**I don't know yet, because Luma Store is only made by me but maybe in the Future there will be more Helpers or an Automation.**

### ****Do I have to submit an Update myself?****

**At the Moment yes, you have to, but if everything goes well there will be an Auto Updater that will do it for you when you publish a new update it will update everything, just look that this will only be for Open-Source Apps exclusively because it will be better to detect them.**
