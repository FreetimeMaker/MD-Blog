### ****Luma Store****

#### ****What's new in Luma Store v4.0.0?****

- **Refined Material You and Liquid Glass throughout the store, including the floating bottom navigation, app details, source management and dialogs**
- **Added automatic light and dark theme switching based on local device time**
- **Added F-Droid index-v2 support with index-v1 fallback**
- **Improved F-Droid app icon loading with multiple fallback paths**
- **Added source health information and last sync status**
- **Added New Apps and Recently Updated sections to Discover**
- **Added an available-updates badge to My Apps and separated updates from already up-to-date apps**
- **Added APK SHA-256 verification and installed-app signature verification before updates**
- **Added package-name verification for downloaded APKs**
- **Added app compatibility information including minimum/target Android SDK, supported architectures and download size**
- **Incompatible Android or CPU architecture builds are now prevented from being installed**
- **Added signing certificate fingerprints, APK hashes and permissions to app details when provided by the repository**
- **Added support for favorites, ignored update versions and per-app source locks in the store data model**
- **Added Anti-Feature explanations so repositories can show why an app has a specific Anti-Feature**
- **Added a developer apps screen: tap a developer in App Details to see their other apps**
- **Added Luma Store app deep-link support**
- **Improved My Apps icon fallback handling**
- **Improved source selection and preferred-source handling**
- **Improved caching for the new F-Droid and app metadata**
- **Extracted new user-visible installer and security messages into translatable string resources**
- **Fixed several Compose compilation issues introduced during the store UI and source improvements**
